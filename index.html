About the studio

Independent by design. Bravely made.

Bravely Made Media is an independent production company founded by Daniel Bergeson, an award-winning filmmaker, producer, and nonprofit leader from Sioux Falls, South Dakota.

The work spans commercials, documentaries, music videos, live performances, and narrative films—always with story at the center.
